Accessibility statement

I want this portfolio to be readable and usable by people with different access needs. This statement describes the current site, its checks, and its remaining limitations.

Features and checks

  • Descriptive links, semantic headings, English language metadata, a skip link, and visible keyboard focus.
  • Scalable text and a flexible layout for smaller screens.
  • A written summary and relationship key accompany the external Kumu map.
  • WebAIM Contrast Checker results: body text #172B40 on white has a 14.42:1 ratio; links #174F86 on white have an 8.4:1 ratio. Both pass WCAG AAA for normal text. The redesigned pages also use a pale cream background, checked separately for readable contrast. Links also use underlines.

Known limitations

The external Kumu map may be difficult to use with assistive technology. The portfolio provides its main findings and complete relationship list in text, but this is not a complete equivalent of every interactive feature. The separate Google My Maps assignment has its submitted reflection and a text overview; map-point details and third-party map controls have not been fully audited. Screen reader testing and a comprehensive accessibility audit have not been completed.

The supplied Make 2, Make 3, Make 4, Make 5, and supply-chain mapping assignments have been converted to web pages. All meaningful assignment images have descriptive alternative text. Homepage cover thumbnails use empty alternative text because each linked card already names its destination. The comic has a six-panel transcript; the Voyant charts have text summaries. Decorative document divider images were omitted. The GIF starts as a still image and plays only when requested, with a Stop animation control. Original Word documents were not altered or certified as accessible. Make 1 and any other earlier-week work not supplied remain outside this review.

Contact

If you encounter a barrier, contact Ty Minton at minton_t1@denison.edu. Please describe the page, the problem, and any browser or assistive technology involved.
