Make 7 · Networks of Knowledge & Power

Behind OpenAI

A network of 27 companies, projects, and facilities connected by 38 relationships. It examines who becomes visible when AI is mapped through corporate infrastructure.

Explore the OpenAI infrastructure network in Kumu

Text description of the network

NVIDIA connects hardware manufacturers, memory suppliers, and systems integrators to the Stargate project. Stargate links investors and technology partners with data center facilities. OpenAI connects to Stargate and cloud providers. NVIDIA has 15 incident edges and 12 distinct neighbors; Stargate has 13 edges and 11 neighbors; OpenAI has seven edges and six neighbors.

Reading the map

Highlighted nodes draw attention to NVIDIA, Stargate, and OpenAI; size does not measure wealth. Supplier links describe NVIDIA’s wider business, not verified deliveries to individual OpenAI facilities. Arrow direction follows the dataset order and does not consistently represent material flow.

What the network leaves out

Workers, mining locations, affected communities, water consumption, and environmental costs are largely absent. Corporate visibility should not be confused with the full range of contributions and consequences behind AI.
